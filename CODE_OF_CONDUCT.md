# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the MyCut project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Be kind, be precise, assume good faith. Critique code, not people. Keep
discussions on-topic and evidence-based — this project's culture is "show
the real output."

## Enforcement
Report issues to conduct@mycut.invalid (placeholder until a real mailbox
exists). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate
this Code of Conduct.
